# Roadmap

Each task here is a choice of the owner. A task leaves this list when its
release is out. The reason for each choice is in
[docs/decisions.md](docs/decisions.md).

## v2.4: Keep up with new chapters

- [#7](https://github.com/Stiven-Gjekaj/Asabunko/issues/7) Check for new chapters in the background
- [#8](https://github.com/Stiven-Gjekaj/Asabunko/issues/8) Tell the user about new chapters
- [#9](https://github.com/Stiven-Gjekaj/Asabunko/issues/9) Show an Updates list
- [#10](https://github.com/Stiven-Gjekaj/Asabunko/issues/10) Download new chapters automatically

## v2.5: The library

- [#11](https://github.com/Stiven-Gjekaj/Asabunko/issues/11) Put books in categories
- [#12](https://github.com/Stiven-Gjekaj/Asabunko/issues/12) Filter and search the library
- [#13](https://github.com/Stiven-Gjekaj/Asabunko/issues/13) Keep a reading history
- [#14](https://github.com/Stiven-Gjekaj/Asabunko/issues/14) Read without a history
- [#15](https://github.com/Stiven-Gjekaj/Asabunko/issues/15) Move many books to a new source at one time
- [#16](https://github.com/Stiven-Gjekaj/Asabunko/issues/16) Manage the storage of downloads

## v2.6: The reader

- [#17](https://github.com/Stiven-Gjekaj/Asabunko/issues/17) Show two pages on a wide screen
- [#18](https://github.com/Stiven-Gjekaj/Asabunko/issues/18) Choose how a page fits
- [#19](https://github.com/Stiven-Gjekaj/Asabunko/issues/19) Keep the reading mode of each series
- [#20](https://github.com/Stiven-Gjekaj/Asabunko/issues/20) Turn pages with the keyboard and choose the tap zones
- [#21](https://github.com/Stiven-Gjekaj/Asabunko/issues/21) Read a novel aloud
- [#22](https://github.com/Stiven-Gjekaj/Asabunko/issues/22) Search in a novel and keep bookmarks and highlights

## v2.7: Tracking

- [#23](https://github.com/Stiven-Gjekaj/Asabunko/issues/23) Track the progress with AniList and MyAnimeList
- [#24](https://github.com/Stiven-Gjekaj/Asabunko/issues/24) Send the progress to Komga and Kavita

## v2.8: The platform

- [#25](https://github.com/Stiven-Gjekaj/Asabunko/issues/25) Translate the app
- [#26](https://github.com/Stiven-Gjekaj/Asabunko/issues/26) Add a widget for continue reading
- [#27](https://github.com/Stiven-Gjekaj/Asabunko/issues/27) Open files from the Files app and share books
- [#28](https://github.com/Stiven-Gjekaj/Asabunko/issues/28) Add haptics and fix the look on iPad

## v3.0: Quality

- [#29](https://github.com/Stiven-Gjekaj/Asabunko/issues/29) Run the full UI suite before each release
- [#30](https://github.com/Stiven-Gjekaj/Asabunko/issues/30) Check the app with VoiceOver and Dynamic Type

## Not on the list

- The search of Toonkotsu gives no titles. This is a fault of the source.
- Mac Catalyst and visionOS. The app is for iPhone and iPad.
- iCloud sync. A sideload with a free Apple ID cannot use iCloud.
